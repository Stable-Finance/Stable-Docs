# Buy Mortgages

## Overview

Stable operates an open market for onchain mortgages where listers are vetted and approved. Mortgages must be tokenized to be listed on Stable's Exchange. Stable takes a small 0.5% fee from the sale proceeds. &#x20;

### Submitting Bids

to submit a bid, check out the mortgage market place and place a bid on an active package or future package



### Buying Mortgages

to buy a mortgage, simply pay the current market price&#x20;
