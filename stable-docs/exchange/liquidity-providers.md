# Liquidity Providers

liquidity providers can provide various stablecoins

#### Stablecoins

* PyUSD
* USDC
* USDT

#### Commodities

* Gold
*

Liquidity providers are returned USDX, the liquidity they provide is used to fund and purchase mortgages. Liquidity providers can stake their USDX in exchange for the SUSDX liquid staking token.
