# USDX

## Real estate stability. Digital asset flexibility.

USDX is a USD-pegged stablecoin backed by a reserve portfolio of U.S. residential mortgages, agency mortgage-backed securities, and liquid reserves. Stable brings the assets, cash flows, and risk controls of mortgage markets onchain for transparent, programmable settlement.

<a href="https://app.trystable.co/mint" class="button primary">Get USDX</a> <a href="https://trystable.co/reserves" class="button secondary">View reserves</a>

{% hint style="success" %}
USDX is designed for 24/7 settlement and verifiable collateral. Contract addresses and supported deployments are maintained in [Official Accounts & Contracts](../../official-accounts.md).
{% endhint %}

## Staked USDX

[Staked USDX](staking-usdx.md), or mUSDX, is a non-rebasing exchange-rate wrapper. Yield retained by the staking vault increases the amount of USDX redeemable per mUSDX rather than changing the holder's token balance.
