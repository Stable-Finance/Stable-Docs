# USDX

## Overview

Stable brought new transparency to mortgage markets through blockchain, enabling 24/7 global transactions and instant settlement. USDX is a USD-pegged stablecoin backed by a reserve portfolio of U.S. residential mortgages, agency mortgage-backed securities (MBS), and liquid reserves — the liquidity layer of those markets.

## Staked USDX

[Staked USDX](staking-usdx.md), or mUSDX, is a yield-bearing token minted to holders who stake their USDX. mUSDX is a non-rebasing, exchange-rate wrapper: its value rises as the underlying reserve portfolio accrues yield, so the amount of USDX redeemable per mUSDX grows over time rather than the token balance rebasing. The yield of mUSDX is the net yield of the mortgage and MBS portfolio backing USDX, after protocol fees.
