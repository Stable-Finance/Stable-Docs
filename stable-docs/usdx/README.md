# USDX

## Overview

Stable brought new transparency to mortgage markets through blockchain, enabling 24/7 global transactions and instant settlement. USDX is the liquidity layer of those markets.

## Staked USDX

[Staked USDX](staking-usdx.md), or mUSDX, is a yield-bearing stablecoin that is minted to depositors who stake their USDX. mUSDX earns yield by providing capital and holding high quality mortgage loans. The yield of mUSDX is the yield of the pool of mortgage loans that are backing USDX.

## Reserves

Stable hosts a Reserves & Transparency page at [trystable.co/reserves](https://trystable.co/reserves) that displays a near real time report on mortgages, reserves, and the health of USDX including links to relevant addresses, collections, and documentation.
