# USDX Whitepaper

## A dollar built on the U.S. mortgage market

The USDX Whitepaper describes how Stable brings mortgage assets onchain and converts their cash flows into transparent, programmable dollar liquidity. It covers issuance, collateral eligibility, staking, peg stability, loss absorption, and the protocol's technical architecture.

**May 2026 · Version 0.2**
