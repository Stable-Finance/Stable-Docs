# Governance

## 4. Governance

RATES holders govern a narrow set of parameters: approved AVM vendors, approved repo counterparties, loan product whitelisting, validator set size, and slashing parameters. Vote weight is stake-weighted with a lock-duration multiplier (ve-style). Governance scope is intentionally limited; operational decisions remain with Stable Company as the licensed operator.

**Emergency response.** Pausing mints, pausing redemptions, halting the autonomous peg-maintenance bot (APMB; [USDX whitepaper §7.4](../usdx-whitepaper/peg-stability.md)), and triggering a loss-waterfall draw are reserved to Stable Company, publicly logged and subject to retrospective review.

**Deferred to a follow-up implementation specification.** Governance procedures — proposal thresholds, voting periods, quorum, timelock specifics, and the precise boundary between RATES governance and operator authority — are specified in a follow-up implementation document published ahead of token distribution.
