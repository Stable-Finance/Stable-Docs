# Abstract

## Abstract

RATES is the protocol token of USDX, with three core utilities: validators stake RATES to attest loan data and gate the minting of new USDX collateral; RATES stakers provide the first-loss safety module backstopping the reserve; and RATES holders govern a narrow set of protocol parameters. This paper specifies its distribution, supply schedule, emissions, validator mechanics, first-loss safety module, and governance in full. USDX itself is specified in the [USDX whitepaper](../usdx-whitepaper/); this document assumes that context.

Detailed operational parameters — validator selection and slashing, delegation mechanics, governance procedures, validator onboarding, treasury management, and cross-chain accounting — are deferred to a follow-up implementation specification published ahead of RATES distribution.

**Keywords:** protocol token, validator staking, first-loss safety module, emissions schedule, governance, mortgage-backed stablecoin.
