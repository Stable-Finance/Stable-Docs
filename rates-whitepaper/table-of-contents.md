# Table of Contents

## Table of Contents

1. **Overview**

> RATES has three core utilities: validator staking that gates new USDX collateral, a first-loss safety module backstopping the reserve, and governance over a narrow set of protocol parameters.

2. **Validator Staking**

> Validators stake RATES to attest the cryptographic integrity of third-party loan data at origination and on a quarterly cadence, earning USDX fees and emissions for verification-of-verification work.

3. **First-Loss Safety Module**

> Staked RATES sits in Tranche 1 of the USDX loss waterfall, slashed first to make USDX holders whole — a distinct activity and stake pool from validating.

4. **Governance**

> RATES governs a narrow set of parameters with ve-style vote weighting; operational and emergency authority is retained by Stable Company as the licensed operator.

5. **Supply, Distribution, and Emissions**

> The canonical specification of RATES supply: 1B genesis supply, a geometrically decaying emissions schedule capping at exactly 2.0B, the emissions split, buyback-and-burn, activity gating, tranche coverage bands, and net supply dynamics.

6. **Topics Deferred to Follow-Up Implementation Specification**

> Operational topics — market-making, validator onboarding and KYC, treasury management, and cross-chain accounting — deferred to a follow-up implementation document.
