### Mortgage NFT Collection

The Mortgage NFT Collection, Stable Home Debt NFTs, are the mortgages that back USDX. Each Stable Home Debt NFT represents a single real estate–backed loan (mortgage) issued and/or tokenized by Stable, and linked to the property held in its corresponding Stable Home Account.

#### Specific to the Solana Deployment

On Solana, the collection is a Metaplex Certified Collection, so every member NFT is verified on-chain against the collection address and cannot claim membership without being signed for by the collection authority. Individual NFTs are derived as PDAs of the USDX Token Program. Members are enumerable programmatically via the Metaplex Digital Asset Standard (DAS) API — `getAssetsByGroup` with the collection address — or by viewing the collection on Solscan or Magic Eden.

## Stable Points

[Stable Points](https://app.trystable.co/points) (SP or points) are a way for users to be recognized for participating in Stable's ecosystem. You can read more about why we built the program [on Medium](https://medium.com/stable-inc/why-we-built-the-stable-points-program-b0398141c0ff).

### Season 2 (live now, through January 31, 2027)

Season 2 began on October 29, 2026 at 06:39 UTC and runs through January 31, 2027. Every Season 1 point carried over in full, and everything earned in Season 2 adds on top. Your [points page](https://app.trystable.co/points) shows both seasons.

| Task                     | Applies To                | Season 2 Reward                         | Notes |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Mint                     | USDX                      | 1 point per $1 minted                   | Example: Minting 100,000 USDX earns 100,000 points. |
| Redeem                   | USDX                      | 1 point per $1 redeemed                 | New in Season 2. Example: Redeeming 10,000 USDX earns 10,000 points. |
| Hold                     | USDX, mUSDX, EURX         | 0.10 points per $1 per day              | Example: Holding 100,000 USDX earns 10,000 points per day. mUSDX is valued at its vault share price. |
| Hold STABLE              | STABLE                    | 0.02 points per $1 per day              | Example: Holding $1,000 of STABLE earns 20 points per day. The holding and burn multipliers below do not apply to these points. |
| Swap                     | USDX, STABLE              | 0.03 points per $1 of volume            | <p>Example: A $1,000 swap into USDX earns 30 points.</p><p>Swap volume must route through one of the <a href="official-accounts.md#stable-points-for-trading-and-liquidity">qualified pools (listed below)</a> to earn points.</p> |
| Liquidity Provision (LP) | USDX, mUSDX, STABLE pools | 1 point per $1 per day                  | <p>Example: An LP position of 100 USDX and 100 USDC earns 200 points per day.</p><p>LP positions must be in one of the <a href="official-accounts.md#stable-points-for-trading-and-liquidity">qualified pools (listed below)</a> to earn points.</p> |
| Refer                    | All actions               | 10% of referred users' Season 2 points  | <p>Referrers earn 10% of the points earned by the users they refer. Referred users keep 100% of their own points.</p><p>Referral points are not multiplied.</p> |

#### Season 2 multipliers

Two multipliers apply to points earned from Solana activity. They multiply each other, so holding 10M STABLE and having burned 10M STABLE gives 9x.

| Tier | 100,000 STABLE | 1,000,000 STABLE | 10,000,000 STABLE |
| ---- | -------------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Holding (STABLE you hold now) | 1.25x | 2x | 3x |
| Burn (STABLE you have burned, ever) | 1.25x | 2x | 3x |

* The highest tier you qualify for applies; tiers are not added together.
* The burn tier is permanent. Only a real burn of STABLE counts; sending STABLE to a dead address does not.
* A multiplier is locked in when points are earned, so points already earned do not change if your tier changes later.
* Activity on other chains earns the base rates above.
* Multipliers do not apply to referral points or to points from holding STABLE.

### Season 1 (ended October 29, 2026)

Season 1 ran from the launch of the points program in 2025 until October 29, 2026 at 06:39 UTC. At the close, 7,013 wallets held 170,433,540 points. Every one of those points carried into Season 2 unchanged.

In Season 1, swaps earned 1% per $1 of volume, holding USDX, mUSDX or EURX earned 10% per $1 per day, minting and LP earned 1 point per $1 (LP per day), redeeming earned nothing, and holding STABLE gave a flat 2x on most points.
